          Architecture Blueprint
          
Viziguide Core Methodology

        

        
      

      

        

          Most of Viziguide doesn't need training a model from scratch. We combine pretrained vision-language models,
          RAG over verified manufacturer manuals, and targeted fine-tuning only where accuracy demands it.

                Approach

The 9-Step Viziguide Roadmap

          

            

              1
              
Narrow Scope

              
5 focused categories: Laptops, Fans, Mixers, Washers, Routers.

            

            

              2
              
Manual Knowledge Base

              
Extract official PDF service manuals, chunk, and index for verified RAG.

            

            

              3
              
Collect Images

              
100-300 images per fault class in varied lighting, plus normal images.

            

            

              4
              
Label with Roboflow

              
Bounding boxes for faulty parts, split 70% train / 15% val / 15% test.

            

            

              5
              
Transfer Learning

              
Fine-tune EfficientNet, ResNet, or YOLO on Google Colab GPU.

            

            

              6
              
Confidence Threshold

              
If confidence is low, ask for clearer angle instead of guessing.

            

            

              7
              
Safety Guardrails

              
Flag high-risk mains/batteries and push to "Find a Professional".

            

            

              8
              
FastAPI Deployment

              
FastAPI /diagnose endpoint with OpenCV 1 FPS frame extraction.

            

            

              9
              
Continuous Improvement

              
User feedback loop records samples to feedback_log.jsonl.

Certified Technician Escalation

        

        
      

      

        

          This fault involves hazardous electrical voltage, high-energy capacitors, or fire hazards.

Authorized OEM Service Centers

            
Recommended for devices under warranty or requiring factory serialized components (Apple Genius Bar, Dell ProSupport, HP Authorized, LG Service).

          

          

            
Local Certified Electronics Bench

            
Look for technicians certified in IPC-7711/7721 (Rework & Repair) or CET (Certified Electronics Technician) equipped with ESD-safe benches and multimeters.
